# Ola — Ride Cancellation Reduction | Product Strategy Case Study

A product management case study focused on improving Ola’s ride booking experience, reducing ride cancellations, improving driver-user trust, and increasing customer retention through smarter UX and personalized recommendations.

## Overview

Ride cancellations create friction for both passengers and drivers. This case study explores the cancellation problem from both sides of the marketplace and proposes a product strategy to reduce avoidable cancellations without changing the existing pricing model.

The case study covers:

- Problem discovery and product outcome mapping
- User survey analysis
- Passenger and driver personas
- Problem validation and market context
- Solution ideation
- RICE-based prioritization
- Final product recommendation
- North Star Metric and supporting metrics
- Product risks and mitigation strategies

## Problem Statement

As part of Ola's Growth team, the objective is to reduce ride cancellations initiated by either the driver or passenger.

**Constraint:** Assume unlimited technology bandwidth, while keeping the current pricing unchanged.

The key question:

> What product initiative should Ola prioritize to meaningfully reduce ride cancellations?

## Research & Problem Discovery

The case study examines cancellation behavior across both sides of the marketplace.

### Passenger Pain Points

Passengers experience:

- Long and uncertain waiting times
- Driver-initiated cancellations
- Last-minute changes to ride availability
- Frustration caused by unreliable pickups
- Cancellation charges in some situations

### Driver Pain Points

Drivers experience:

- Uncertainty about the profitability of a destination
- Unattractive drop-off locations
- Waiting time between rides
- Lost earning opportunities
- Incentive-related decision making

A key insight from the analysis is that some driver cancellations are influenced by uncertainty about the drop-off location and its earning potential.

## User Research

The case study uses survey responses to understand:

- Ride frequency
- Cancellation frequency
- Driver-vs-passenger cancellation patterns
- Reasons for cancellation
- Waiting-time frustration
- Driver motivations and pain points

### User Personas

**Aastha — Passenger**

A college student who depends on ride-hailing for regular transportation and values reliability, affordability, and reaching destinations on time.

**Rajesh — Driver**

A full-time driver who values steady earnings, flexibility, and incentives, while being sensitive to unproductive trips and difficult drop-off locations.

## Solution Space

Four product concepts were evaluated:

1. **Drop-off Statistics**
2. **Gamifying the Waiting Experience**
3. **Driver Score**
4. **Cancellation Timer**

## Prioritization

The proposed solutions were evaluated using the **RICE framework**.

| Solution | Reach | Impact | Confidence | Effort | RICE Score | Priority |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Drop-off Statistics | 100% | 5 | 100% | 3 | 1.67 | P1 |
| Cancellation Timer | 100% | 4 | 80% | 2 | 1.60 | P2 |
| Driver Score | 100% | 3 | 70% | 3 | 0.70 | P3 |
| Gamify Waiting Experience | 100% | 3 | 70% | 4 | 0.525 | P4 |

## Chosen Solution

### Providing Drop-off Location Statistics for Drivers

The proposed feature gives drivers additional information about a ride's destination before accepting the trip.

Example insights include:

- **Probability of getting another ride** from the destination area
- **Average fare** in that area
- Contextual information about the destination at that time of day

### Value to Drivers

- Better-informed ride decisions
- Greater visibility into destination earning potential
- Reduced uncertainty
- Potentially improved earnings

### Value to Passengers

- Fewer driver-initiated cancellations
- More reliable rides
- Better overall booking experience
- Reduced risk of being left without transportation

## Success Metrics

### North Star Metric

**Reduced Driver Cancellation Rate**

Percentage decrease in driver-initiated ride cancellations after the feature is introduced.

### Supporting Metrics

- Ride completion rate
- Driver engagement with drop-off statistics
- Customer ratings and feedback
- Driver behavior change
- Passenger waiting time
- Retention of high-performing drivers
- Cancellation-related operational cost savings

## Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Drivers become overly dependent on the feature | Position the information as a supplement to navigation and decision making |
| Incorrect or outdated location data | Continuously monitor and improve data accuracy |
| Drivers do not trust the feature | Provide onboarding, education, and feedback loops |
| Information becomes distracting | Keep the interface simple and prioritize the most useful information |
| Misuse of destination information | Establish clear policies and monitoring |
| Privacy or regulatory concerns | Review the feature with legal and compliance teams |
